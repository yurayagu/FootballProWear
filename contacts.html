Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the USA and 7-10 business days to Europe. Express shipping options are available at checkout for faster delivery.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn items in original packaging. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship to the USA and all European countries. We are working on expanding our shipping destinations in the near future.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your package on our website or the carrier website.

Do you offer team discounts?

Yes! We offer special pricing for team orders. Please contact us at info@footballprowear.com with details about your team size and needs.
